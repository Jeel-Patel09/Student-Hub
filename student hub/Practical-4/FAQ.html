Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I register on StudentHub?

Click on the Register page, fill in the required details, and submit the form.

2. How can I log in?

Use your registered email address and password to log in.

3. How can I update my profile?

Go to the Profile page, edit your details, and click the Update Profile button.

4. Where can I check upcoming events?

Visit the Events page to see all upcoming college activities and schedules.

5. How do I contact the administrator?

Use the Contact page to send your message or query to the administrator.
